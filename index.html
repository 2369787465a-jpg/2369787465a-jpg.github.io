CAADRL / CluPDTSP

This project develops CAADRL for pickup-and-delivery problems with pairing and precedence constraints in spatially clustered instances. Global self-attention and intra-cluster attention capture multiscale structure; a hierarchical decoder handles route selection within and between clusters.

Multi-view learning · Mental health

Accepted

Learning from incomplete views.

MC-TRCM

This project proposes MC-TRCM to fuse heterogeneous mobile and wearable features under substantial missingness. Feature-source tokens and missing-aware gating feed a conditioned Transformer; recursive decoding refines predictions across six regression and classification tasks on DepreST-CAT and PSYCHE-D.

Project leader · Advisor: Prof. Guangyu Zou, DUT

Empirical asset pricing & beta prediction

Jul 2025 – Jan 2026

Project leader · Advisor: Prof. Shuai Qiao, DUT

Studied the MAX anomaly in China’s A-share market using CSMAR data, portfolio sorting, Fama–MacBeth regressions, multifactor risk adjustment, and Newey–West inference; explored machine learning for dynamic beta prediction.

High-resolution ozone downscaling

Oct – Dec 2025

Project leader · Advisor: Prof. Libin Jiao, DUT

Combined GEOS-Chem, ERA5, and ground observations on a 0.01° × 0.01° grid. Compared tree and neural models with cross-validation, Model Confidence Set testing, and SHAP analysis of atmospheric-chemistry consistency.

Cybercrime indicators & policy evaluation

Jan – May 2025

Project leader

Analyzed crime, reporting, and prosecution indicators from 110 countries. Combined K-means, LSTM counterfactuals, and multi-period difference-in-differences to evaluate policies in China, Germany, and the United States. Published at CIPAE 2025.
